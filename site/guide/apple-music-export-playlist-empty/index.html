Why your Apple Music playlist export is empty

Published 31 October 2026.

You chose File › Library › Export Playlist…, picked M3U, and opened the file. It says #EXTM3U and nothing else. Nothing's broken; here's what's going on.

Friday Warm-Up.m3u #EXTM3U
…and that's the whole file.

The short answer: the songs aren't files on your Mac

An M3U playlist is a list of file paths. Apple Music can only write a path for a song that's stored on your Mac as a file. If your library syncs with iCloud, through an Apple Music subscription or iTunes Match, most songs live in the cloud and have no local file, so Apple Music quietly leaves them out.

When none of the songs are downloaded, the export comes out with just the header. When some are, you get a playlist with gaps.

Songs downloaded from an Apple Music subscription are protected, so even when they do appear in the export, DJ software like rekordbox can't play them.

The fixes

1. Download the songs first

Download the playlist in Apple Music (the download button, or right-click › Download), wait for it to finish, then export again. This fills in the paths for music you own, like purchases and uploaded files. It won't help with subscription songs, which stay protected.

2. Export as Unicode Text instead

Choose File › Library › Export Playlist… and set Format to Unicode Text. This export lists every song, cloud or not, with its name, artist, album and length, just without file paths. It's handy as a shopping list, or as input for a tool that can match songs to your own files.

3. Match the playlist to your DJ files

If what you really want is the playlist in rekordbox, you don't need Apple's files at all. CrateMate is a free Mac app that reads your Apple Music playlists directly, finds each song in your DJ folder by artist, title, version and length, and writes an .m3u8 that points at your own files. rekordbox links each entry to the track already in your collection, cues and grids included.

It reads the playlist straight from Apple Music, so there's nothing to export. If it can't get access to your library, open a Unicode Text export from fix 2 with File › Open Playlist Export… (⌘O).
